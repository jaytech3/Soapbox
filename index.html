Users do not read the pricing page.

Twelve weeks of public Groww reviews, clustered into themes, with the one charge users keep saying they did not expect. Out of it comes a weekly note for the product team and a factual explanation support can paste, neither of which reaches an internal tool until a person approves it.

    Five themes, ranked by volume

    Which charge is misunderstood, not merely disliked

    A review counts as confused when it names the charge and says it was not expected: hidden, extra, undisclosed, bina bataye, pata nahi. Volume alone would always pick the best-known charge, so the score multiplies how often a charge is raised by how often it is misread.
