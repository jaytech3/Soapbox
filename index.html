    How this run went, step by step

      Every number on this site is computed from data/reviews.csv by src/engine.js, in your browser, with no model in the loop. The same file always produces the same note.

      Every review, in one place

      All the reviews with text from the twelve-week window. Filter by theme, rating or a word, and read them as the users wrote them. Display names were dropped at ingest; ids are hashed.

      Five themes, and the words that mark them

      A review goes to its strongest theme only, so nothing is counted twice. Reviews that match nothing are left out rather than forced into the nearest bucket. The distinguishing words are the data's own, picked by log-odds against the rest of the corpus.

      Three quotes that carry the week

      verbatim, spelling included; one per week of the window

      Which charge is misunderstood, not merely disliked

      A review counts as confused when it names a charge and says it was not expected: hidden, extra, undisclosed, bina bataye, pata nahi. Volume alone would always pick the best-known charge, so the score multiplies how often a charge is raised by how often it is misread.
