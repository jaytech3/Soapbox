Week ending …

Every review is a speech.
Here is the plan.

Soapbox reads every public review from the last twelve weeks, sorts the speeches into five themes, and finds the one charge people keep saying they never expected. Out of it come a weekly note for the product team and an explanation support can paste. Neither reaches a tool until you approve it.

      Reviews

      Every review with text from the window, as the users wrote them. Display names were dropped at ingest; ids are hashed.

      Themes

      A review goes to its strongest theme only, so nothing is counted twice. Reviews that match nothing are left out rather than forced into a bucket. The distinguishing words are the data's own, picked by log-odds against the rest of the corpus.

      Three quotes that carry the week

      verbatim, spelling included · one per week of the window

      Fee issue

      A review counts as confused when it names a charge and says it was not expected: hidden, extra, undisclosed, bina bataye, pata nahi. Volume alone would always pick the best-known charge, so the score multiplies how often a charge is raised by how often it is misread.
