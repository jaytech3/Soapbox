# Weekly product pulse — 12 Oct 2026

**Scope.** 12,765 Google Play reviews for Groww, 21 Jul 2026 to 12 Oct 2026; 3,220 carried text and were analysed.

**Top themes**

1. **Easy to start, easy to use** — 1299 reviews, 40.3%, 4.68★.
2. **Orders and app reliability** — 382 reviews, 11.9%, 2.18★.
3. **Support that does not close the loop** — 361 reviews, 11.2%, 2.06★.

**What users said**

> "they are say less brokerage charge more very bad experience…" — 1★, 6 Aug 2026

> "Aware Hidden charges, very high commission. in a equity trading…" — 1★, 5 Aug 2026

> "On viewing the chart, the bottom navigation buttons are hidden…" — 1★, 28 Aug 2026

**Key observation.** Orders and app reliability leads the negative themes (382 reviews, 2.18★). The most fixable signal sits one rank below, in charges (#4, 237 reviews): 13 of the 110 reviews naming brokerage also say they did not expect what was taken — "hidden", "extra", "bina bataye", and several attribute the whole deduction to brokerage alone. Every charge is published, so this is placement, not pricing. Mentions are flat week on week.

**Three things to do**

1. Itemise the deduction on the order confirmation, not only the next day's contract note: brokerage, DP charge, CDSL, GST.
2. Show the rupee figure live as quantity is entered, including the ₹0 case below ₹100 of debit value.
3. Ship the fee explainer below as the canned support reply, and track repeat contacts on charges.


</details>

**Explanation issued to support**

- Brokerage on an equity order is ₹20 or 0.1% of the order value, whichever is lower, subject to a minimum of ₹5. It is charged per executed order, so a buy and a later sell are two separate charges.
- A delivery sell also attracts a DP (depository participant) charge, because shares leave your demat account: ₹16.50 charged by Groww and ₹3.50 charged by the depository, CDSL. The depository part is ₹3.25 where the first holder is a woman. Buying and intraday trading do not attract it.
- The DP charge is a flat amount per sell transaction, not a percentage, so a ₹500 sell and a ₹5,00,000 sell attract the same amount. Where the debit value is under ₹100, Groww's ₹16.50 is not applied.
- GST of 18% applies on brokerage, DP charges and exchange transaction charges. It is a tax, collected on top of those charges rather than retained as a fee.
- STT, stamp duty, exchange transaction charges and SEBI turnover fees are levied by the government, the exchange or the regulator. They appear on the same contract note but are not Groww's charges.
- Every one of these lines is itemised on the contract note and in the ledger for the trade date, and the rates are published on Groww's pricing page.

**Sources**

- https://groww.in/pricing
- https://groww.in/help/stocks/searchable/when-are-dp-charges-levied
